# Content Needed From Axe Leadership

## Company and brand

- Approved company description
- Company history, service area, and leadership information
- Approved brand colors, including the final accent color
- Primary, reversed, icon, and vector logo files
- Brand usage guidance

## Services and capabilities

- Actual services and capability categories
- Equipment, capacities, materials, processes, and certifications that may be stated publicly
- Geographic service area and project-type priorities

## Careers

- Current openings
- Pay ranges
- Confirmed benefits
- Shift schedules
- Training and advancement language that can be supported
- Qualifications and hiring requirements
- Careers-routing email
- Application retention requirements

## Contact and quotes

- Public contact details
- Office/shop address
- Quote-routing email
- Information required for a quote request
- Approved response-time language

## Media and privacy

- Approved media and usage restrictions
- Employee photo/likeness policy
- Customer/site approval rules for project media
- Axe Media Drop review and publishing workflow
- Privacy-policy requirements
- Cookie and analytics preferences
- Retention schedules
- Legal contact for privacy requests
